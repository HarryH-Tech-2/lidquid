Lidquid, for journalists

Everything you need for a story. Reply within one working day.

in one paragraph

Lidquid pays people to put a removable QR sticker for a brand they approve on the back of their laptop. Strangers in cafés, libraries and coworking spaces scan it out of curiosity, the brand pays per verified scan, and the laptop owner earns a share. There's no posting and no follower count. Lidquid is in pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, with cities opening in order of waitlist demand.

Key facts

WhatA marketplace that rents laptop-lid ad space to brands, paid per verified QR scan
Who it's forStudents, remote workers and café regulars (owners); local businesses, apps and consumer brands (advertisers)
StatusPre-launch pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. No earnings figures published yet.
Cost to ownersFree. Owners never pay Lidquid anything.
How owners are paidPer verified scan, at a rate set by the brand and approved by the owner before the sticker ships
Verified scanA real person on a real phone scanning a sticker for the first time; repeat-device scans, self-scans and bots are excluded
StickerRemovable vinyl made for laptop lids; peels off without residue
