Fair questions.

the stuff everyone asks before joining
How much can I actually earn?
It depends on where your laptop lives. Each brand sets a rate per verified scan, and a lid that sits in a busy café five days a week earns far more than one that stays home. We'll publish real pilot numbers once the first city has run — no made-up projections.
Will the sticker wreck my laptop?
No. We use removable vinyl designed for laptop lids — the same class of material as premium decals. It peels off clean, leaves zero residue, and doesn't care about your stickers-underneath situation because it goes on top of bare lid only.
Do I get a say in which brand goes on my lid?
You approve every brand before anything ships. You see the design, the brand, and the rate — and you can say no for any reason, or swap out at the end of a campaign. It's your lid; we're just the agent.
What counts as a "verified scan"?
A real person, on a real phone, scanning your QR for the first time. Repeat scans from the same device, bots, and — sorry — you scanning it yourself in a mirror don't count. Verification is how brands trust the numbers enough to pay properly.
When does my city open?
Cities open in order of waitlist demand — joining the list literally is the vote. When your city crosses the threshold, everyone there gets one email, and pilot spots go to the top of the list first.
Do I have to do anything once the sticker's on?
That's the whole point — no. No posting, no follower count, no promoting. You work from your café like you already do; curiosity does the rest. The only thing you might have to do is answer "wait, what is that?" from the table next to you.
What if I want out?
Peel it off. Done. No lock-in, no minimum term, no fees. You keep everything you've earned up to that day.
