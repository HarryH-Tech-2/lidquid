The non-USD versions of square, pin and poster assets are old campaign drafts. Prefer the `-usd` files or regenerate the asset before posting.

## Pinterest Quick Specs

- 2:3 at 1000x1500 is the recommended ratio; square assets are also usable.
- Title max: 100 chars. Description max: 500 chars.
- Always include a tracked `?ref=` link.
- Pinterest Tag is installed on the site, so signups from pins are tracked as conversions.
