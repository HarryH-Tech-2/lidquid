# Reply bank, 2026-11-11

1. **"Is QR code advertising worth it for a small business?"** Only if you can count it. Give each placement its own code, count unique devices rather than total scans, and divide everything you spent (printing and design included) by unique scans. Guide: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/cost-per-scan-advertising?ref=rd-20261111

2. **"Can I make money in cafés without a job?"** Small amounts, honestly: remote work and freelancing are the real earners; some ad-style options pay you for what you already carry. Be wary of anything charging a fee up front. Ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261111

3. **"What's the difference between CPM, CPC and cost per scan?"** CPM is per thousand impressions, CPC per click, cost per scan per QR scan: someone saw the ad in the physical world and acted. I'm building Lidquid, a pre-launch pilot that bills this way, so I'm biased. Explainer: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/cost-per-scan-advertising?ref=rd-20261111
