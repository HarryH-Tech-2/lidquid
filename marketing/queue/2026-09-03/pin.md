## title

Nobody puts a logo on your laptop but you — rent your lid, keep the veto

## description

Lidquid rents the back of your laptop to brands, but nothing ships until you say
yes. You see the brand, the design and the rate per verified scan first — then
you approve it, or you don't. No is a whole sentence. It's removable vinyl, it
peels off clean, and there's no posting, no followers, no promoting. Strangers
scan the QR out of curiosity and you earn $ per verified
scan. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist
demand, so joining is the vote for yours.
