## title

## description
You have never once looked at the back of your own laptop. Everyone sitting behind you in the cafe has. Lidquid rents that blank lid to a brand you approve — one removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting, and it peels off clean whenever you are done. A pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, cities opening in order of waitlist demand. Join the waitlist and vote your city up.
