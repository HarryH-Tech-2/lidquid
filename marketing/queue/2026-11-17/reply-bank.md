**1. "How do pay-per-scan QR ads stop people scanning their own code over and over?"**
The usual approach: count only the first scan per device, filter self-scans (the owner's own phone or linked devices), reject bots and hold odd bursts for review. Repeat scanning from a friend's phone still counts once, so there's nothing to gain. Detail: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261117

**2. "Is a QR code sticker on a laptop in a café worth anything to advertisers?"**
Only if the scans are real people. Look for programs that show raw vs rejected vs verified numbers openly, and that don't treat shared café wi-fi as one person. Dwell time is the upside, since people sit in view for hours. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261117

**3. "Any side hustle in cafés that isn't a scam?"**
Test it: money should only flow to you, nothing up front, and the payout formula should be explicit. Per-scan pay is one example, and it's in pilot only (no earnings promised); how scans are counted is here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261117
