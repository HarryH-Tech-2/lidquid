## LinkedIn
Pay-per-scan advertising only works if "a scan" means something. In our pilot design, a verified scan is the first scan from a real device: repeats, self-scans and bots are filtered, unusual bursts are held for review, and a shared café network is not treated as a duplicate, since dozens of genuine strangers really do share it. Brands see raw, rejected and verified counts side by side. Lidquid is a pre-launch pilot (50 laptops, one city at a time) and we have no results to publish yet.
