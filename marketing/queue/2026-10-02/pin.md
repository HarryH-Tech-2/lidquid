## title
You never see the back of your laptop. The whole café does.

## description
The back of your laptop is the one surface you never look at. The person at the next table looks at it all day. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker that earns you $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. Curiosity does the work. Peels off clean, zero residue. Right now it's a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, and cities open in order of waitlist demand — so joining is the vote. Free to join.
