## title
the back of your laptop is doing nothing

## description
You carry a flat blank rectangle into a café and point it at a room full of strangers for six hours. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker that says "I get paid every time you scan this." People scan out of curiosity. You earn $ per verified scan: real person, real phone, first time only. No posting, no followers, no promoting. Peels off clean, zero residue. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand.
