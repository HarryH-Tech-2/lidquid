## title
strangers keep talking to my laptop (and it pays)

## description
Turns out the back of your laptop is the most sociable thing you own. Lidquid rents your lid to a brand you approve, and people in the café lean over to scan the QR out of pure nosiness — you get paid per verified scan, they get an answer to "wait, what is that?". No posting. No followers. No promoting. Just you, in your usual seat, with a removable vinyl sticker that peels off clean. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining is the vote. lidquid.com
