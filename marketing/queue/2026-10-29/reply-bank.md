**1. "Is it rude to work in a café for hours?"**
Not if you behave like a customer: buy something roughly every hour or two, give up the big table at peak times, take calls outside and don't camp on the only outlet. Off-peak hours make it much easier. More on café etiquette and cost per working hour: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261029

**2. "Coworking day pass or café, which is cheaper?"**
Depends on hours. For two or three hours a café usually wins. For a full day with calls, a day pass that includes coffee and wifi can be equal or cheaper. Work out drinks + food + transport vs pass + transport for your own city. The math is laid out here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261029

**3. "Any way to earn a bit from the time I already spend in cafés?"**
Local clients are the real one: regulars see you often, and café owners need freelance help. A smaller option is a removable QR sticker ad on your lid, paid per verified scan. I'm building one (Lidquid), it's in pilot with no proven earnings, so treat it as a maybe. Overview: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261029
