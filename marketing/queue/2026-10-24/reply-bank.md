**1. "Do QR code stickers actually get scanned?"**
Only when the person has time and a reason. Dwell time matters: a code someone sees for hours at a café table gets scanned far more than one they walk past. A clear payoff or a bit of curiosity helps. Full playbook: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/qr-code-sticker-marketing-for-local-businesses?ref=rd-20261024

**2. "Any side hustle I can do while sitting in a café?"**
Freelance work, tutoring, or renting out space you already have. One low-effort option is a sticker ad on your laptop lid, paid per scan; I'm building one (Lidquid), in pilot, so no proven earnings yet. Honest comparison: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261024

**3. "How do I track results from a QR code?"**
Use a dynamic code or a short link you control, a separate one per placement, and add UTM parameters. Treat basic scan counts as an upper bound because repeats are included. https://www.lidquid.com/guides/cost-per-scan-advertising?ref=rd-20261024
