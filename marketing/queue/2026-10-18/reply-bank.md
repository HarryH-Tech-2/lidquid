## 1. "Is advertising on my car worth the hassle?"
Depends on how much you drive. Reports put legit programs at a few hundred a month at best, and you need an install and a clean removal. If you're low-mileage, smaller formats (decals, stickers) are less hassle but pay less. I'm building a laptop-sticker version (small pilot), side-by-side here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising-vs-car-wrap?ref=rd-20261018

## 2. "Do laptop stickers damage the laptop?"
Removable vinyl made for this usually peels off clean if it hasn't baked in heat for years; the risk is cheap adhesive. Test a corner, avoid the screen side and don't leave one on for years. Longer answer on heat, residue and resale: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261018

## 3. "How do small businesses use QR stickers?"
Cheapest wins: one unique, trackable URL per placement so you know which sticker gets scans, and a clear reason to scan (offer, menu, signup). Avoid generic homepage links. Playbook: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/qr-code-sticker-marketing-for-local-businesses?ref=rd-20261018
