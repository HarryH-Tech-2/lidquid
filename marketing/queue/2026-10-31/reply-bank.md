**1. "Should I use a static or dynamic QR code on printed stickers?"**
If you want to count scans or change the destination later, dynamic. Static is fine for something permanent like a wifi password. The catch with dynamic is that the redirect depends on the service, so keep the account active. More on the tradeoffs: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/qr-code-sticker-marketing-for-local-businesses?ref=rd-20261031

**2. "Can you make money letting a business put a QR sticker on your stuff?"**
Maybe, but check who pays, what for, and how scans are verified. Walk away from upfront fees. I'm building one (Lidquid); it's a pilot with no proven earnings, so treat it as a maybe. Checklist: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261031

**3. "Where should a café or shop put QR codes so people actually scan them?"**
Where people wait with their hands free: the counter, the table, the queue. Give a reason to scan (a discount, a menu) and keep the landing page mobile-first. Placement and offers: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/qr-code-sticker-marketing-for-local-businesses?ref=rd-20261031
