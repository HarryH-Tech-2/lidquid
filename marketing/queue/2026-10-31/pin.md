**angle:** static vs dynamic QR: why a sticker that can be counted beats one that can't.

- **title (≤100):** static vs dynamic qr code stickers: which one can you actually measure?
- **description (≤500):** a static qr code can't tell you who scanned it. a dynamic one points at a tracking link, so you can count scans, change the offer and spot junk traffic. this is the reasoning behind lidquid, a pilot that pays people to carry a removable qr sticker on their laptop lid, paid per verified scan. 50 laptops, one city at a time. no earnings data yet. guide: static vs dynamic, placement, offers and measuring.
