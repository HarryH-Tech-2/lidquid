## title

my lid has a bouncer — laptop ad space you approve first

## description

Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand — and nothing ships until you say yes.
You see the brand, the design and the rate per verified scan first, then approve it or turn it
down, for any reason. Removable vinyl, zero residue, peel it off anytime. No posting, no
followers, no promoting: strangers in cafés scan the QR out of curiosity and you earn $ per
verified scan. A pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist
demand, so joining is your vote.
