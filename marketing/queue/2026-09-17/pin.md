Your lid faces the entire room — and it's been advertising nothing this whole time. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve, as a removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers scan it out of curiosity; you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. Peels off clean, zero residue. Right now it's a pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time — and cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is literally your vote. Free to join.
