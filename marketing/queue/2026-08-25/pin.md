## title
the back of your laptop is unemployed — rent your lid as ad space

## description
lidquid rents the back of your laptop to a brand you approve. a removable vinyl QR sticker goes on your lid, strangers in cafés scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan — a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. no posting, no followers, no promoting: you work from your café like you already do. peels off clean, zero residue, say no to any brand for any reason. it's a pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time, cities open in order of waitlist demand.
