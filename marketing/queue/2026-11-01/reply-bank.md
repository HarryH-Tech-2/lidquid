1. **"Is a QR code on a flyer worth it? How do I know?"** Count it like any channel: total cost (printing, design, tool, time) divided by unique scans, then see what share of scans turn into customers. If a scan is worth more than it costs, keep going. Give each placement its own code or you can't tell which one worked. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/cost-per-scan-advertising?ref=rd-20261101

2. **"Are laptop stickers safe/okay for my laptop?"** Removable vinyl is generally fine on most lids; the usual risk is cheap adhesive left on for months. Peel slowly, warm it with a hairdryer, and test a corner first. Full answer: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261101
