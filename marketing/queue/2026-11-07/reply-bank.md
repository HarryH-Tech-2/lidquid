# Reply bank, 2026-11-07

1. **"I got a cheque for a car wrap ad but it's more than they said and they want me to send the rest to the installer. Legit?"** No, that's the classic overpayment scam. The cheque looks real and may even clear provisionally, then bounces days later, and the money you sent is gone. A real advertiser never needs you to forward part of a payment. Don't deposit it or send anything, and report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Red flags: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261107

2. **"How can I tell if a side hustle is a scam before signing up?"** Ask one question: where does the money come from, and can they say it in one sentence? Then check whether you have to pay first, move money through your account, or recruit people. Search the company name plus "scam" and "complaints", and read how and when you get paid. Checklist: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261107

3. **"Got a text offering money for liking videos or rating products. Real?"** It's almost certainly a task scam. Small payments arrive early to build trust, then you're asked to deposit your own money to unlock bigger tasks or withdraw. That deposit is the scam. Stop replying, don't send anything, and report the number. Patterns and what to do if you've paid: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261107
