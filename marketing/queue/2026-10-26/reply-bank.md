**1. "Do laptop stickers actually earn anything / is it a scam?"**
Be sceptical of anything that asks for money up front or promises fixed monthly numbers. The honest version pays per verified scan, so earnings depend on how often your laptop is out in public. Lidquid is in pilot and has no earnings data yet; any figure you see from us is an illustration only (5 days × 3 scans × $0.50 ≈ $32/month). I run it, so judge accordingly. Breakdown of how to tell: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261026

**2. "How do you advertise to college students cheaply?"**
Posters and society partnerships are cheapest, but put a QR with its own tracked link on each so you can see what worked. Check campus posting rules first; fly-posting gets brands banned. Channel comparison: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/campus-advertising-to-reach-students?ref=rd-20261026

**3. "How do I measure whether my QR code ads work?"**
One QR per placement, each with its own UTM, then compare cost per scan or sign-up across channels. Make the code big, high contrast, and test at the real viewing distance. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/cost-per-scan-advertising?ref=rd-20261026
