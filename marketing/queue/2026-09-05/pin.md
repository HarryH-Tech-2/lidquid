## title

## description
The lid of your laptop faces away from you all day. It faces everyone else. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve, sticks a removable vinyl QR on it, and pays you $ per verified scan when a stranger gets curious. No posting, no followers, no promoting — you sit in the café like you already do. It peels off clean, zero residue, and you can say no to any brand for any reason. It's a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, cities open by waitlist demand. Join the list and vote for yours.
