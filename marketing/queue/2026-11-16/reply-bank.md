**1. "Will a sticker on my MacBook lid make it overheat or damage it?"**
No: the lid holds the screen, and the fans and vents are in the base or near the hinge. Keep stickers off vents, hinges, speaker grilles and the keyboard, use proper vinyl, and test a hidden corner if the finish is soft-touch. Details: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261116

**2. "Do stickers leave residue or hurt resale value?"**
Good removable vinyl usually peels off clean: warm it with a hair dryer, peel slowly at a low angle, and wipe any film with isopropyl alcohol on a cloth. Cheap paper stickers and very old ones are the ones that leave patches. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261116

**3. "Do stickers void the warranty?"**
Generally a sticker on the outer lid doesn't affect a hardware warranty, but policies differ, so check your manufacturer's terms. Avoid covering vents or anything that blocks service access. The guide covers it with the usual caveats: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261116
