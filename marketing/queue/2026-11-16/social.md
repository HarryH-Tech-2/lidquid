Title: Does a sticker on the lid really cause overheating or lower resale value?
Body: Quick summary of what I found researching this: on almost all laptops the lid is just the screen and its cover, and heat leaves through vents in the base, side or hinge area, so a thin sticker on the outer lid isn't trapping meaningful heat. The practical rules are to keep stickers off vents, hinges, speaker grilles and the keyboard deck, use real vinyl rather than paper, and warm it gently with a hair dryer before peeling; isopropyl alcohol clears leftover film. Resale: a clean peel usually leaves nothing, though an old sticker can leave a faint tone difference where sun hit the uncovered lid.
