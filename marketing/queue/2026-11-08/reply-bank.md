1. **"If I put a QR sticker on my laptop, can people track me from it?"** Not if it's set up properly. A plain QR code just opens a link, and the owner's name isn't in it. The thing to check is what the destination page collects. With Lidquid the scanner sees the brand's content, not the owner's details, and owners only see a count of verified scans. How it works: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261108

2. **"Are QR codes on stickers safe to scan?"** Mostly, but look before you scan: is it stuck on top of another sticker, does the preview URL match what it claims, and does the page ask for logins or payment? Peel-over stickers are the usual trick. If the preview looks odd, skip it. Guide to how QR codes get used (and misused): https://www.lidquid.com/guides/qr-code-sticker-marketing-for-local-businesses?ref=rd-20261108

3. **"Is there any way to earn from my laptop in a café without being on camera or posting?"** A few: freelancing at the café itself, or renting your lid as ad space. The lid option pays per verified scan from strangers, with no posting or followers. It's in pilot with no earnings numbers yet, so treat it as a maybe, not a plan. Café-work ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261108
