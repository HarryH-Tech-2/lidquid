**1. "What can I rent out to make passive income without buying anything?"**
Start with what's idle: a parking spot or driveway (check lease/HOA first), storage space, gear you rarely use. Be wary of anything that asks you to pay first. Rough work-per-dollar rundown: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-from-things-you-already-own?ref=rd-20261027

**2. "Is renting out a spare room actually passive?"**
Not really. Messages, cleaning, local rules and guests make it closer to running a tiny hotel. Parking and storage are far lighter. Same guide covers the comparison: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-from-things-you-already-own?ref=rd-20261027

**3. "Any passive income for students with no savings?"**
Look for things you already own and carry. One low-effort option is a removable sticker ad on your laptop paid per scan; I'm building one (Lidquid), in pilot, so no proven earnings yet. More ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-ideas-with-no-money?ref=rd-20261027
