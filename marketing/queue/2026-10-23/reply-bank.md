**1. "Cheap guerrilla marketing for a coffee shop / local business?"**
Stuff that costs little and is countable: a QR code with its own tracking link on every flyer or cup sleeve, a loyalty offer only reachable by scan, and partnerships with the shop next door. Count scans per placement for a month and drop what doesn't work. More on measuring placements: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising?ref=rd-20261023

**2. "Do people actually scan QR codes on stickers?"**
Sometimes, when there's a clear reason: a concrete payoff ("free first class") beats "learn more", and the code should be big, high-contrast and send people to a fast mobile page. Expect small numbers; test before you scale. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising?ref=rd-20261023

**3. "How can I reach students or remote workers offline?"**
Go where they sit for hours: libraries, student unions, cafés, coworking. Stickers, table cards and a QR offer all work. I work on Lidquid, a pilot that sponsors laptop-lid stickers (owners opt in, pay per verified scan; no published results yet), so take that as one option among several. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising?ref=rd-20261023
