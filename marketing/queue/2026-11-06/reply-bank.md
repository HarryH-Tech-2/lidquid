# Reply bank, 2026-11-06

1. **"Is there any way to get paid for doing literally nothing?"** Not literally. What exists is getting paid for something you'd do anyway or letting someone use something you aren't using: cashback on normal spending, a spare parking spot, savings interest. Expect small, uneven amounts, and treat anything promising big fixed money as a scam script. Honest rundown: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/get-paid-to-do-nothing?ref=rd-20261106

2. **"Are bandwidth-sharing apps safe?"** Mixed. Other people's traffic leaves through your IP address, it may break your ISP's terms, and some apps have had security issues. Read the privacy policy, check your ISP terms and never run one on a work network or device. Trade-offs listed here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/get-paid-to-do-nothing?ref=rd-20261106

3. **"Can you really get paid to put ads on your stuff?"** Sometimes, but be wary of fixed, high weekly pay regardless of where you go, and anything that sends a cheque or asks for a fee. Legit models pay per verified result and let you stop whenever you like. Scam checklist: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261106
