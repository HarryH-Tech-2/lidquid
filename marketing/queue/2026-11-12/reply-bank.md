# Reply bank, 2026-11-12

1. **"Can I get paid to advertise if I don't have a car?"** Car programs generally want a car and a clean driving record, so no car usually means no luck there. Some newer ideas use a laptop or other things you already carry instead. Whatever you try, it should never charge you up front. I'm building Lidquid, a pre-launch pilot of the laptop version, so I'm biased. Explainer: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/get-paid-to-put-a-sticker-on-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261112

2. **"Are those 'sticker on your laptop' ads a scam?"** Check who pays whom. Real ones pay you and ask for nothing; scams send a cheque and ask you to forward some of it, or charge a fee to start. Also check you can peel it off whenever you like. More red flags: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261112

3. **"Is there a lower-commitment side hustle for café work?"** If you're already in a café for hours, the lowest-commitment options are the ones that pay for what you already do. Remote work earns the most; ad-style options pay little and are pilots at best. Ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261112
