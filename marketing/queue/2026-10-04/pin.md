## title
the back of your laptop is the only billboard you own

## description
You look at the front of your laptop all day. Strangers only ever see the back. Lidquid rents that side to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker goes on your lid, people in cafés scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan: a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. Peels off clean, zero residue. It's a pilot for now — 50 laptops, one city at a time, cities opening in order of waitlist demand.
