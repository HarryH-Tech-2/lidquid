## The digest's 3 suggestions

1. **Run the "verified scan" angle next** — real person, real phone, first scan only. It is the only major site claim never used in marketing, and it is the one that answers the "can't I just scan it myself?" objection.
2. **Stop generating a new angle daily; cut one week of the winners instead.** With 30 days banked and zero shipped, the bottleneck is review, not supply. One PR of seven distinct-angle pieces is far easier to approve than 30 separate PRs.
3. **Build a reusable brand pin template in Canva** so each day only swaps the headline text. Every run so far has asked the AI generator for the riso/zine look and every run has come back off-brief — today needing a hand-patched watermark.
