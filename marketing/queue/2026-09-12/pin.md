## title
"wait — what IS that?" the laptop sticker that pays you to be asked

## description
Your laptop lid is the one surface you never look at. Lidquid rents it to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker — and you earn $ per verified scan (real person, real phone, first time).

The part nobody expects: it talks to people. The freelancer two tables over. The barista. The stranger who asks what it is and becomes a coffee mate. A tiny billboard that breaks the ice.

No posting, no followers, no promoting. Peels off clean.

Pilot: 50 laptops. Cities open by waitlist demand.
