1. **"How do QR code ad programs know the scans are real?"** Good ones count only the first scan per device, drop the owner's own scans, filter bots and hold odd bursts for review. Ask whether the program shows rejected scans, not just the total. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261102

2. **"Is getting paid to put an ad on my laptop legit or a scam?"** Real ones are free to join, never ask you to pay or cash a cheque, and tell you what counts as a paid action. Lidquid is a pre-launch pilot with no earnings published yet, so treat any number as illustrative. Checklist: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261102 (disclose if you're affiliated.)

3. **"Can I make money working from cafés?"** Small amounts, honestly. Time there is worth more if it's also doing a second job, but be wary of anything promising big passive income. Ideas and red flags: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261102
