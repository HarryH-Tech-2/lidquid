**title**
dead space: the one part of your laptop that could be earning

**description**
You look at your screen all day. You never look at the back of your laptop lid. It's the only surface you own that's doing nothing.

Lidquid rents that lid to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers in cafés scan it out of curiosity. You earn $ per verified scan: real person, real phone, first time.

No posting. No followers. Peels off clean, zero residue.

Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand — joining is the vote.
