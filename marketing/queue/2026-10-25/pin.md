**Title (62):** get paid per scan: a laptop sticker that only earns when it works

**Description (~380):** most ads pay for being looked at. lidquid pays for being scanned. a removable QR sticker for a brand you approved sits on your laptop lid, and you earn when a real stranger scans it (one verified scan per person, repeats and bots filtered). free, no posting, no followers. illustration only: 5 days x 3 scans x $0.50 x 4.33 is about $32 a month. pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, opening by waitlist demand.
