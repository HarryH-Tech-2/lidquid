**1. "What's a good cost per scan for a QR code campaign?"**
There's no universal number. Divide total cost by scans, then look at what scans turn into (visits, sign-ups). A cheap scan that never converts is worse than a pricier one that does. Formula and worked example: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/cost-per-scan-advertising?ref=rd-20261025

**2. "Are QR code scan counts reliable?"**
Treat raw counts as an upper bound. They usually include repeats, self-scans and bots. Ask whether the number is deduplicated before comparing it with anything. More on that: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261025

**3. "Is there any side hustle that pays for actual results rather than time?"**
Performance-based gigs exist (affiliate, referral, per-action ads). Check who pays, for exactly what, and how it's verified. I'm building one (Lidquid, laptop-lid QR stickers, paid per verified scan); it's a pilot, so no proven earnings yet.
