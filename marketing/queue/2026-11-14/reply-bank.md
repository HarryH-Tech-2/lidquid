**1. "Is the laptop sticker advertising thing a scam?"**
The idea is real, but the format is easy to fake. Test: a real program pays you and never asks you to pay, cash a cheque or send money. Ask which brand, how you're paid and how to stop. I'm building Lidquid (pilot, no earnings published), and the checklist is here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261114

**2. "Got a cheque for more than I'm owed for advertising on my car, should I deposit it?"**
No. Overpayment cheques that ask you to forward the difference are the classic scam: the cheque bounces days later and the money you sent is gone. Contact your bank and report it to your consumer protection agency (in the US, reportfraud.ftc.gov). More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261114

**3. "How do I tell a real side income from a fake one?"**
Follow the money: it should only flow toward you, the payout should be explained as a formula, and you should be able to quit without a fee. Anything urgent ("only 3 spots left") or unnamed is a pass. Longer list: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261114
