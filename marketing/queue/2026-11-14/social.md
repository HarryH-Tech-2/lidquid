Title: 7 questions I'd ask before joining any "get paid to advertise" program
Body: Car wraps, phone cases, laptop stickers: the format is old and scammers love it. Before you say yes: 1) do I ever pay anything (fees, kits)? 2) who is the brand, and can I see the design? 3) how exactly am I paid, and at what rate? 4) what counts toward earnings, and how are fake or repeat actions handled? 5) how and when do I get paid out? 6) how do I stop, any minimum term or penalty? 7) will it damage my stuff? Never deposit a cheque and send part of it anywhere. Disclosure: I'm building one of these (Lidquid, still a pilot), so I'm biased, but the list works on anyone.
