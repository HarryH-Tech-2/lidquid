## title
the laptop sticker that pays you every time a stranger asks about it

## description
Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers in cafés scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. The side effect nobody expects: the freelancer two tables over finally talks to you. Peels off clean, zero residue. Pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote. Add your email and your city.
