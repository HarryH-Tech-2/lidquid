# Reply bank, 2026-11-04

1. **"What passive income can I start with no money?"** Be honest that it's rarely passive at first: the realistic zero-budget options cost time (writing, a small digital product) or use something you already own (spare room, parking, ad space on a car or laptop). Skip anything that asks for a fee first. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-ideas-with-no-money?ref=rd-20261104

2. **"How do I tell a legit 'get paid to' site from a scam?"** Ask who pays, for exactly what, and how it's verified. Legit programs pay you; scams ask you to pay, buy gift cards or forward part of a cheque. Checklist: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261104

3. **"Can a student earn money with just a laptop?"** Yes, but pick things you can pause during exams, and ignore anything that needs an upfront purchase. Ideas with the scam flags marked: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/side-hustles-for-students?ref=rd-20261104 (disclose if you're affiliated.)
