Disclose you're the founder wherever Lidquid is mentioned.

**1. "Easy side hustle for a student with a laptop and no money?"**
Skip anything that asks you to pay first. Paid research studies (check your psych department's page), user testing and campus jobs cost nothing and flex around classes. None is a big earner. We compared ten here, scam flags included: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/side-hustles-for-students?ref=rd-20261021

**2. "Do those get-paid-to-advertise offers work or are they scams?"**
Some are real, many aren't. Red flags: a fee to start, a cheque you're told to forward, a guaranteed income. Real programs don't charge you. I run Lidquid, which is in pilot with no earnings published yet, so I'd rather you check the red flags first: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261021

**3. "How do brands track QR code ads?"**
Each code gets its own tracked link, so a brand can see scans per placement. Quality is the hard part (repeat scans, bots), which is why we only count verified first scans. Explainer: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261021
