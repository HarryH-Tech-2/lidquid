## 1. "Is getting paid to put an ad on my car/laptop a scam?"
Can be, because the format is a classic. The tell is direction of money: a real program pays you and never asks you to pay, deposit a cheque and forward part of it, or buy gift cards. Ask which brand, what the rate is, how earnings are counted and how you stop. Vague answers are your answer. I'm building a laptop-sticker version (still a small pilot) and wrote the checklist here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261017

## 2. "Side hustles I can do from a café without posting online?"
Honestly, most are real work (freelancing, tutoring, data entry). Passive options are thin. One low-effort option is renting the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve, paid per verified QR scan. It's early (pilot, no earnings figures yet), so treat it as a small extra, not income. More on working from cafés: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261017

## 3. "How do QR code ads get measured?"
Per scan, ideally filtered to unique real people: a tracked redirect URL counts scans, then you drop repeats, the owner's own scans and bots. Pay on verified scans instead of impressions and both sides can check the numbers. Longer explainer: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261017
