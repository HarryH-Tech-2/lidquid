## Instagram
legit or scam? the one-line test.

"get paid to advertise on your stuff" is one of the oldest scam formats there is. the car-wrap version goes: they send a cheque that's too big, you forward the difference, the cheque bounces, the money's gone.

so: a real program pays you. it never asks you to pay, cash a cheque, buy a gift card or send anything anywhere. it names the brand up front. it lets you say no.

**Title:** The "get paid to advertise" scam checklist (works for car wraps, laptop stickers, anything)
**Body:** Short version: if an offer ever asks you to pay a fee, deposit a cheque larger than what you're owed, or send money via gift card, wire or crypto, walk away. Real programs pay you and nothing more. Other tells: guaranteed high weekly pay, no named brand, pressure to reply today, asks for bank details before you've seen an offer. Questions worth asking in writing: do I ever pay anything, which brand and can I see the design first, how exactly am I paid, how do I stop. If you've already sent money, call your bank straight away and report it (FTC in the US: reportfraud.ftc.gov).
