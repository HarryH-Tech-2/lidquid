## title
i approved this ad: rent your laptop lid to a brand you actually picked

## description
most side hustles want your face, your followers, or your evenings. lidquid just wants the back of your laptop. a removable vinyl QR sticker goes on your lid, strangers in cafés scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. you see the brand, the design and the rate before anything ships — and you can say no, for any reason. peels off clean, zero residue. no posting, no promoting. a pilot for now: 50 laptops, one city at a time, cities opening by waitlist demand.
