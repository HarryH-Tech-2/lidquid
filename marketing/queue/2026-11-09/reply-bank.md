1. **"Can I get paid to advertise on my phone case or water bottle?"** Occasionally, but mostly not. Few programs exist for small items because they're hard to verify and rarely seen. Anything that asks you to pay a fee or deposit first is a red flag. Walkthrough of what's real: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/get-paid-to-advertise-on-your-phone-case?ref=rd-20261109

2. **"What's a side hustle I can do from a café without posting online?"** Freelance work at the table is the classic one. A lower-effort extra is renting your laptop lid as ad space: a removable QR sticker, paid per verified scan. It's in pilot with no earnings numbers yet, so treat it as a maybe. Café ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261109

3. **"Do QR code stickers actually get scanned?"** Placement decides it. Eye level, a clear reason to scan and a short wait nearby (like a café queue) help most. Test on a phone first and give each placement its own link so you can count. Basics: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/qr-code-sticker-marketing-for-local-businesses?ref=rd-20261109
