## description

You look at one side of your laptop all day. The other side faces the whole café — and it's blank. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve, sticks a removable vinyl QR on it, and pays you $ per verified scan when a curious stranger scans it. No posting. No followers. No promoting. Peel it off whenever you want and keep what you've earned. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote.
