**title**

**description**
The back of your laptop lid is the only screen you never look at — and the only one strangers do. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve, as a removable vinyl QR sticker. Someone in the café gets curious, scans, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting. No followers. No promoting. You sit where you already sit. The sticker peels off clean, zero residue, and you can say no to any brand for any reason. Currently a pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time, cities open in order of waitlist demand. Join the waitlist and vote for yours.
