# Reply bank, 2026-11-05

1. **"What's the easiest passive income for a college student?"** The ones that piggyback on things you already do: renting out gear or books you've finished with, selling a template of your own study system, or ad space on the laptop you study on. None replace a job; treat them as a top-up and label how passive each really is. Eight ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-for-college-students?ref=rd-20261105

2. **"Is it okay to sell my class notes online?"** Depends on your university. Many restrict selling course materials, and lecture slides usually belong to the lecturer. Sell only your own original work and read your academic integrity policy first. More student-safe ideas: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-for-college-students?ref=rd-20261105

3. **"Which 'brand ambassador' offers for students are scams?"** Anything that sends a cheque and asks you to forward part of it, charges for a starter kit, or pays mainly for recruiting other students. Checklist: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/passive-income-scams?ref=rd-20261105
