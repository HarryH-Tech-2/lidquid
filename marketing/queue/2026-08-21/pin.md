## title

## description
the back of your laptop lid is the one surface you never look at. everyone behind you in the café looks at nothing else. lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker — and you earn $ per verified scan: a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. no posting, no followers, no promoting. peels off clean, no lock-in. it's a pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time, and cities open in order of waitlist demand. email + city is the whole ask.
