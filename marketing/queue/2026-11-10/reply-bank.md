1. **"Skin or sticker for a MacBook?"** Both protect from scuffs and neither makes the laptop run hot (the lid isn't where it vents). Skins cover everything but are a slower peel; stickers are quick and cheap, but pick proper vinyl, not paper. Just don't cover vents or speaker grilles. Comparison: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-skins-vs-stickers?ref=rd-20261110

2. **"How do I remove old laptop stickers without leaving residue?"** Warm the surface gently with a hair dryer, lift a corner and peel slowly at a low angle. If film is left, isopropyl alcohol on a cloth usually clears it. Test a hidden corner first on soft-touch or painted finishes. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261110

3. **"Can you get paid to put a sticker on your laptop?"** Sometimes, but be careful: a legit one never asks you to pay or forward money.
