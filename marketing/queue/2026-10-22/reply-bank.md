**1. "How often should I buy something when working in a café?"**
Rule of thumb: something when you arrive, then something every hour or two, more often at peak times. Give up big tables when it's busy and take calls outside. Being a regular the staff like keeps the arrangement easy for everyone. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261022

**2. "Is coworking cheaper than working from cafés?"**
Depends on hours. Short sessions: café usually wins. Full 8-hour days: a day pass that includes coffee and wifi can match or beat it. Do the math with your own city's prices. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261022

**3. "Any way to earn a bit from time I spend in cafés anyway?"**
A few: loyalty programs lower your cost per hour, and being a regular can lead to local freelance work. There are also pilots that pay for a QR sticker on your laptop lid per verified scan (I work on one, Lidquid; it's in pilot, with no real earnings published yet, so treat numbers as estimates). More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261022
