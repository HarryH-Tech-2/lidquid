**title**

The back of your laptop is unemployed — put your lid to work

**description**

Your laptop lid is the one surface you never look at, and the only one everybody behind you does. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker that goes on your lid. Strangers scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. Peels off clean whenever you want, zero residue. Right now it's a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, and cities open in order of waitlist demand — so joining the list is your vote. Free to join.
