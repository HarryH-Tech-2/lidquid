Title: Before you pay a listing fee to sell sticker space on your laptop, who exactly will see it?
Body: There are a handful of "get paid to advertise on your stuff" formats: car wraps, lid marketplaces, auctions, branded gear. They differ mostly in what you have to bring. Auctions (like the MacBook sticker one this summer) pay for the owner's audience, so they're one-offs for people with a following. Marketplaces often charge a listing fee whether or not anything sells. A fair test: can I name who will see my laptop and why a brand would pay to reach them? If not, don't pay to wait.
