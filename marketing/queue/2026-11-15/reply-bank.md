**1. "Can you actually get paid to put ads on your laptop?"**
Yes, a few formats exist: marketplaces that sell fixed sticker spots, auctions (mostly for people with an audience), and pay-per-scan programs. I'm building one of the latter (Lidquid, pilot, no earnings published yet), so I'm biased. Whatever you pick, it shouldn't cost you anything up front. Comparison: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising-vs-car-wrap?ref=rd-20261115

**2. "Is a car wrap worth it, or is it a scam?"**
Real programs exist but spots are limited, you need a car you drive a lot, and fake-cheque scams are everywhere: never deposit a cheque and send part of it on. If you don't drive much, a removable laptop sticker is a lower-commitment alternative. Side by side: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising-vs-car-wrap?ref=rd-20261115

**3. "Site wants a listing fee to sell ad space on my laptop, worth it?"**
Only if you can say who will see your laptop and why a company would pay to reach them. Fees are due whether or not anything sells, so it's a bet on your own audience. If you have no following, look for programs that are free to join and pay per result. More: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising-vs-car-wrap?ref=rd-20261115
