## title

Which City Gets Paid First? Rent Your Laptop Lid as Ad Space

## description

Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker that strangers scan out of curiosity in cafés. You earn $ per verified scan: a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. It's a pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time — and cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote for yours. Peels off clean, zero residue, no lock-in. Add your email and city and find out if yours goes first.
