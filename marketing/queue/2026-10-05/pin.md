## title
The back of your laptop is the only ad space you own

## description
Every laptop has a side its owner never looks at. Lidquid rents that side to a brand — one removable vinyl QR sticker, and you earn $ per verified scan when a stranger in the cafe scans it out of curiosity.

No posting, no followers, no promoting. You approve every brand before anything ships, and the sticker peels off clean with zero residue.

Pilot stage: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote. Free to join.
