## description

Strangers scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan: a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. It peels off clean, zero residue. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand. Join the list and vote for yours.
