## title

## description
The back of your laptop is the one surface you never look at, and the only one strangers do. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve, as a removable vinyl QR sticker. People scan it out of curiosity in cafés, and you earn $ per verified scan — real person, real phone, first-time scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. It peels off clean whenever you want. Currently a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, opening in order of waitlist demand. Join the list and vote for your city.
