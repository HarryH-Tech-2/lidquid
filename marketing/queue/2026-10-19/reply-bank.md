## 1. "Do laptop stickers leave residue?"
Cheap adhesive does; removable vinyl mostly doesn't. Heat and time are what bake it on, so avoid vents and don't leave one for years. Warm it gently (hairdryer) before peeling and test a corner first. More on heat, residue and resale: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/are-laptop-stickers-bad-for-your-laptop?ref=rd-20261019

## 2. "Are 'get paid to advertise' apps legit or scams?"
Real ones pay you and tell you who the advertiser is and how you're paid. Scams ask for a fee, or send a cheque and ask you to forward part of it. I'm building one in this space (small pilot), checklist here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/is-laptop-sticker-advertising-legit?ref=rd-20261019

## 3. "What can I do in cafés to make a bit of money without a laptop job?"
Be honest: most options are small. Selling to people in the room is hard; passive ones use what's already there. Tutoring or freelancing is real work; renting ad space on your lid is lower effort but pays less and depends on foot traffic. Options laid out here: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261019
