## description

```
The back of your laptop has been blank this whole time. Lidquid rents that space
to a brand you approve, posts you a removable vinyl QR sticker, and pays you $ per
verified scan — a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting, no
followers, no promoting. You sit in your usual café like you already do and
curiosity does the rest. Peels off clean, zero residue. Pilot: 50 laptops, one
city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining is the vote.
```
