## description
The back of your laptop is the one surface you never look at — and the only one strangers do. Lidquid rents it to a brand you approve, as one removable vinyl QR sticker. Someone at the next table scans out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. It peels off clean, zero residue, and you can say no to any brand. It's a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, and cities open in order of waitlist demand. Join the list — that's the vote.
