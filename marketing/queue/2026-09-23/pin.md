**title**
the back of your laptop is doing nothing (so we rent it out)

**description**
It's the only screen you never look at — and it faces the whole café. Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve. A removable vinyl QR sticker goes on, strangers scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan: a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. Peels off clean, zero residue, and you can say no to any brand, any reason. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand.
