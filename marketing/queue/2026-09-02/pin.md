## title
Your Laptop Has a Blank Side. Brands Will Pay For It.

*(52 characters)*

## description
There's a flat, empty, permanently-facing-outward rectangle on the back of your laptop. You have never looked at it. Everyone in the café has.

Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve, as a removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers scan it out of curiosity. You earn $ per verified scan — a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan.

No posting. No followers. No promoting. It peels off clean.

Currently a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote. Add your city →
