## title
Your lid is doing nothing — rent the back of your laptop to a brand

## description
The back of your laptop is the one surface you never look at. Everyone sitting behind you in the café does. Lidquid rents that space to a brand you approve: one removable vinyl QR sticker, and you earn $ per verified scan when a stranger scans it out of curiosity. No posting, no followers, no promoting. It peels off clean, zero residue. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote. Add your email and city.
