Your laptop lid is dead space — rent it out to a brand you approve

## description
the back of your laptop is the one bit of real estate you own and never actually look at. lidquid rents it to a brand you approve — a removable vinyl QR sticker that peels off clean, zero residue. strangers scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. no posting, no followers, no promoting: you work from your café like you already do. currently a pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time. cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote.
