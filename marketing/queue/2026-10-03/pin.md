## title
every other sticker on your laptop is a freeloader

## description
Think about the stickers already on your laptop. The conference one. The vinyl you paid for on Etsy. The band, the bookshop, the little mountain. Every one of them cost you something, and not one has ever paid you back. Lidquid is the sticker that pays rent — a removable vinyl QR, from a brand you approved, earning you $ per verified scan when a curious stranger scans it in a café. No posting, no followers. Peels off clean. Pilot: 50 laptops, cities open by waitlist demand.
