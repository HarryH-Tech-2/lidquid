## title
wait — what IS that? the laptop sticker that pays you when strangers scan it

## description
Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve. A removable vinyl QR sticker goes on, strangers in cafés scan it out of pure curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan — a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting. No followers. No promoting anything to anyone. The sticker peels off clean and leaves zero residue. Right now it's a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote.
