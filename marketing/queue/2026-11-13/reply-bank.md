**1. "Can you really make money putting a sticker on your laptop?"**
Maybe a little, and it depends on how often the laptop is in busy public places. A rough way to estimate: days out × new scans per day × rate × 4.33. Five days, three scans, $0.50 is about $32/month as an illustration. I'm building Lidquid (pilot, no real earnings published yet), and the guide lays out the math: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/how-much-can-you-earn-from-laptop-advertising?ref=rd-20261113

**2. "What side hustles work if I'm in a café all day anyway?"**
Look for things that fit into time you already spend there: freelancing, tutoring, or low-effort add-ons. Be wary of anything that wants a fee up front. One low-effort add-on is paid laptop stickers, which are small money but near-zero effort. Longer list: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/make-money-working-from-cafes?ref=rd-20261113

**3. "Are QR code ads on stickers measurable?"**
Yes, if each sticker has its own tracked link, you can count unique scans and filter repeats. The weak point is fraud, such as the owner scanning their own code, so look for first-scan-per-device rules. That's why Lidquid counts "verified" scans only: https://www.lidquid.com/guides/what-is-a-verified-scan?ref=rd-20261113
