## title
The side of your laptop you never see is the side that pays

## description
You stare at one side of your laptop all day. The café stares at the other. That back panel is the most-looked-at, least-used surface you own. Lidquid rents it to a brand you approve, as a removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting — you work from your café like you already do. Peels off clean, zero residue, no lock-in. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand.
