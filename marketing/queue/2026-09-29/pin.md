## title
my laptop made a friend (and got paid for it)

## description
here's the bit nobody warns you about. you put a small QR sticker on your laptop lid, go to your usual cafe, and someone two tables over leans across: "wait, what is that?" that's a conversation. it's also a verified scan, which is the part that pays you. lidquid rents the back of your laptop to a brand you approve. removable vinyl, peels off clean, zero residue. no posting, no followers, no promoting. you sit there being a cafe regular, same as always. pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time.
