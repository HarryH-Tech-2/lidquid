We built Lidquid to rent the back of a laptop lid to brands. Owners earn $ per verified scan.

The thing we didn't design for: the sticker starts conversations.

A QR on a lid is a question mark at eye level. People in cafés ask about it — and the person answering is a regular who'd sat quietly in that room for months.

Attention that someone opted into, in a place they already are, is a different product from attention you interrupt. It turns out the same is true of the conversations.
