## title

## description
The back of your laptop is the only real estate you own and never look at. Lidquid rents it to a brand you approve — one removable vinyl QR sticker on your lid. Strangers in cafés scan out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. You see the brand, design and rate before anything ships, and can say no for any reason. Peels off clean. Pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time — cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining the list is the vote.
