## title
which city gets the first 50 laptops? you vote by joining the waitlist

## description
Lidquid is a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time. Which city goes first isn't picked in an office — cities open in order of waitlist demand, so adding your email and your city is literally the vote for yours. Here's what you're voting for: a removable vinyl QR sticker on the back of your laptop lid, from a brand you approve before anything ships. Strangers in cafés scan it out of curiosity and you earn $ per verified scan — real person, real phone, first-time scan. No posting, no followers. Peels off clean.
