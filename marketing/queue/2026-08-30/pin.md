## title
no posting. no followers. just a sticker on your laptop.

## description
Every side hustle wants you to make content. This one wants you to sit still. Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve, as a removable vinyl QR sticker. Strangers in cafés scan it because they're nosy, and you earn $ per verified scan — a real person, a real phone, a first-time scan. No posting. No follower count. No promoting. It peels off clean whenever you're done. Currently a pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, and cities open in order of waitlist demand.
