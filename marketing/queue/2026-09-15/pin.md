## title
wait, what is that? — the laptop sticker that pays you per scan

## description
Lidquid rents the back of your laptop lid to a brand you approve. A removable vinyl QR sticker goes on, strangers in the café scan it out of curiosity, and you earn $ per verified scan — real person, real phone, first-time scan. No posting, no followers, no promoting. It peels off clean, zero residue. The side effect nobody expects: people lean over and ask about it, so quiet regulars become people you actually know. A pilot — 50 laptops, one city at a time, opening in order of waitlist demand.
