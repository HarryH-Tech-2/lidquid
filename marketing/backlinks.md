# Lidquid backlink plan

Status on 2026-10-16: lidquid.com has effectively no backlinks. That's the main thing holding back the 18 guides. Work this list top to bottom. Every placement should link to the **most relevant guide**, not just the homepage. Track results in Search Console → Links.

Ground rule for every pitch: be honest that Lidquid is a pre-launch pilot (50 laptops, one city at a time). Never claim users, earnings, scans or partners that don't exist. Reporters check, and "honest pilot" is itself a good angle.

---

## Tier 1: highest value, do this week

### 1. Ride the Brand My Mac story (tech press)
In August 2026 a Bordeaux developer (Vincent, @VynseDev) auctioned 10 sticker spots on a MacBook he hadn't bought yet. TechSpot and Mezha covered it. Mezha noted earlier versions of the idea (Jonathan Wilke's Outbid campaigns, BrandMyTesla). Journalists who covered it have already shown they find "laptop as ad space" newsworthy.
- Targets: the [TechSpot article](https://www.techspot.com/news/113653-developer-auctions-off-sticker-space-macbook-doesnt-own.html) and the [Mezha article](https://mezha.ua/en/news/brand-my-mac-314613/). Pitch the bylined writers via the outlets' tips/contact pages. Also pitch other tech and indie sites that cover quirky internet business (look for who covered BrandMyTesla).
- Angle: *"Brand My Mac worked because one developer had an audience. Lidquid is trying to make it work for anyone with a laptop in a café: you approve the brand, and you're paid per verified scan instead of a one-off auction."*
- Link to: `/guides/laptop-sticker-advertising-vs-car-wrap` (it already covers the auction) or the homepage.

## Tier 3: compounding (monthly)

- **Make one piece of real data.** Reporters link to original numbers. Example: spend a week in 10 cafés and count open laptops, blank lids vs stickered lids, and hours per seat. Then publish "We counted X laptops in Y cafés" as a guide. It's honest fieldwork no one else has, and it fits roundups and journorequests. (Don't publish it until the data is real.)
- **University resource pages.** Many colleges have "student money / part-time work / avoid scams" pages. `/guides/passive-income-scams` and `/guides/passive-income-for-college-students` are useful enough to suggest politely to financial aid or careers offices.
- **Podcasts.** Side-hustle and indie-founder podcasts take guests. Pitch the "odd idea, honest pilot" story. Podcast show notes usually link.
- **Reddit and Quora** (links are mostly nofollow, but they bring traffic and AI tools cite them). Answer real questions in r/sidehustle, r/beermoney, r/digitalnomad and your city or university subreddits. Disclose that you're the founder. The daily routine drafts these in `marketing/queue/*/reply-bank.md`.
- **Unlinked mentions.** Once press happens, google `"lidquid" -site:lidquid.com` monthly and ask anyone who mentions you without a link to add one.

## Listing kit (copy-paste)

- **Name:** Lidquid
- **Tagline (60):** Get paid to put a brand's QR sticker on your laptop lid
- **Short (160):** Lidquid rents the back of your laptop to brands you approve. Strangers scan the QR sticker, brands pay per verified scan, and you earn. Free, no followers needed.
- **Long:** Every open laptop in a café, library or coworking space shows a blank lid to the room for hours. Lidquid turns that into ad space the owner controls. You approve the brand, design and rate, stick on a removable vinyl QR sticker, and earn every time a new person scans it. Brands pay only for verified scans (real people, real phones, first scan), not impressions. Lidquid is in pilot: 50 laptops, one city at a time, with cities opening in order of waitlist demand.
