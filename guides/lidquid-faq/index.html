What is Lidquid? Every question, answered in one place

By the Lidquid team · Updated October 28, 2026 · 4 min read

the short answer

Lidquid is a marketplace that rents the back of your laptop to a brand you approve. You put a removable QR sticker on your lid, strangers in cafés, libraries and coworking spaces scan it out of curiosity, the brand pays per verified scan, and you earn a share. It's free to join, there's no posting and no follower count, and you can peel the sticker off any time.

  • Cost to you: nothing, ever
  • How you're paid: per verified scan, at a rate you approve before anything ships
  • Status: pre-launch pilot, 50 laptops, one city at a time

What is Lidquid, in one paragraph?

Lidquid turns the back of an open laptop into a small, measurable billboard. Laptop owners stick on a removable vinyl QR sticker for a brand they've approved. When a stranger scans it, Lidquid checks that the scan is real, the brand pays for it, and the owner earns. Brands get owner-approved placements in places with hours of dwell time, and they pay only for verified attention instead of impressions nobody can prove.

How does Lidquid work, step by step?

  1. Join the waitlist. Leave your email and city. Cities open in order of waitlist demand, so joining is your vote.
  2. Approve a brand. When your city opens, you see the brand, the sticker design and the rate per scan. Say yes, or say no for any reason.
  3. Stick it on your lid. A removable vinyl sticker arrives in the post. Peel, stick, and work from your usual café.
  4. Earn per verified scan. Every real first-time scan pays you. Want out? Peel it off and keep what you've earned.

How much does Lidquid pay?

Each brand sets a rate per verified scan, and you approve it before anything ships. What you earn depends on how often your laptop is out in public and how busy those places are: a lid in a busy café five days a week earns far more than one that stays at home.

Lidquid hasn't published real earnings yet, because the pilot hasn't finished its first city. Any figures on this site are labeled as illustrations. We'll publish real pilot numbers once we have them. For the maths behind an estimate, read how much can you earn from laptop advertising?

What counts as a verified scan?

A verified scan is a real person, on a real phone, scanning your sticker for the first time. Repeat scans from the same device, bots and your own scans are filtered out. Verification is what lets brands trust the numbers enough to pay per scan. More detail in what is a verified scan?

Is Lidquid legit, or a scam?

Lidquid is free for laptop owners and never asks you to pay, cash a cheque or send money anywhere. That matters, because "get paid to advertise on your stuff" is a well-known scam format: the car wrap scam sends a fake cheque and asks you to forward part of it. With Lidquid, money only ever moves toward you, and you see the brand and rate before anything ships.

See the full red-flag checklist in is laptop sticker advertising legit? and passive income scams to avoid.

Will the sticker damage my laptop?

No. Lidquid uses removable vinyl made for laptop lids. It goes on the bare lid, away from vents, ports and hinges, and peels off without residue. Read are laptop stickers bad for your laptop? for what's safe on aluminum and plastic lids.

Who is Lidquid for?

Laptop owners: students, remote workers, freelancers and café regulars whose laptops spend hours open in public. You don't need followers or a social media account.

Brands: local businesses, apps and consumer brands that want to reach people in cafés, campuses and coworking spaces, and want to pay per verified scan rather than per impression. Brands can see raw scans and billable scans side by side, including what was rejected and why. Start with laptop sticker advertising for brands.

Do I have to do anything once the sticker is on?

No. There's no posting, no follower count and no promoting. You work where you already work. The only thing you might have to do is answer "wait, what is that?" from the next table.

When does Lidquid open in my city?

Lidquid is in a pre-launch pilot with 50 laptops, opening one city at a time. Cities open in order of waitlist demand. When your city crosses the threshold, everyone on the list there gets one email, and pilot spots go to the top of the list first.

How do I stop?

Peel the sticker off. There's no lock-in, no minimum term and no fee to leave, and you keep everything you've earned up to that day. You can also swap brands at the end of a campaign.

How is Lidquid different from other ways to get paid to advertise?

Car wraps need a car and a long commitment, and are a favorite scam format. Laptop lid marketplaces and sticker-spot auctions usually need an audience, or charge you a listing fee with no guaranteed buyer. Lidquid needs neither: it's free, it pays per verified scan, and it works for people with no following. See the side-by-side in laptop sticker advertising vs car wraps and other options.

How do I contact Lidquid?

Laptop owners can join the waitlist below. Brands can request pilot pricing, and we reply within two working days. Journalists can use the press kit, which has key facts, photos and an inquiry form.
