Laptop sticker advertising vs car wraps and other ways to get paid to advertise

By the Lidquid team · Updated October 16, 2026 · 4 min read

the short answer

Laptop stickers are the lowest-effort, lowest-commitment way to get paid to advertise. There's no upfront cost and no audience needed, and the sticker peels off whenever you like. Car wraps pay more per placement when they're real, but you need a car, a lot of driving and some caution, because the category is full of scams. Marketplaces and auctions favor people who already have a following.

What are the main ways to get paid to advertise on your stuff?

There are five common ones: sponsored laptop stickers, car wraps and decals, laptop lid marketplaces, sticker-space auctions and branded clothing or gear. They differ mainly in who pays, what you're paid for and how much you need to bring (a car, a following, money up front).

  • Sponsored laptop stickers (like Lidquid): a brand you approve puts a QR sticker on your lid, and you're paid per verified scan.
  • Car wraps and decals: a brand covers some or all of your car, and you're paid a fixed monthly amount for driving.
  • Laptop lid marketplaces: you list your lid and companies buy fixed-price sticker spots.
  • Sticker-space auctions: you auction spots on your laptop, usually to fans or followers.
  • Branded clothing and gear: brand ambassador programs that pay in free products, discount codes or commission.

How does laptop sticker advertising work?

You put a removable QR sticker for an approved brand on your laptop lid, and you're paid each time a new person scans it. With Lidquid there's nothing to buy and nothing to post, and you see the brand, design and rate before the sticker ships.

The strengths are commitment and effort, because there's almost none of either. You keep working from your café, library or coworking desk as usual. The sticker is removable vinyl that peels off without residue, and you can stop any time and keep what you've earned. The trade-off is that pay depends on real scans, so a laptop that rarely leaves home earns very little. See how much laptop advertising earns for worked examples.

Are car wrap advertising programs worth it?

Real car wrap programs exist and can pay a fixed monthly amount, but they're selective, they usually want drivers who cover a lot of miles in specific areas, and the category is a magnet for scams. If you drive a lot for work, it can be worth applying to well-established programs, carefully.

The main downsides:

  • You need a car, and usually one that's in good condition and driven a lot.
  • Spots are limited. Many people sign up and never get matched with a campaign.
  • Wraps are a bigger commitment. They take professional installation and removal, and you may need to keep them on for a set term.
  • Scams are everywhere. The fake-cheque "car wrap" scam is one of the best-known advertising scams. The FTC and other consumer agencies have warned about it repeatedly.
Rule of thumb: a real program never sends you a cheque and asks you to forward part of it to an installer. If that happens, it's a scam. Our scam checklist covers the other warning signs.

What about laptop lid marketplaces?

Some marketplaces let you list your laptop lid and sell fixed-price sticker spots to companies. They usually charge a listing fee, which you pay whether or not anyone buys. They can work if you have a following or a visible niche, since buyers are often paying for your audience as much as your lid.

Before paying a listing fee, ask yourself who will actually see your laptop and why a company would want to reach them. If you don't have a clear answer, you're paying to wait.

Can you auction sticker space on your laptop?

Yes, and it can work well for people with an audience. In August 2026, TechSpot reported on a developer who auctioned sticker spots on a MacBook he hadn't even bought yet, using the proceeds to fund the laptop. Sponsors in deals like this are mostly paying for the creator's reach and the novelty, so they're one-off wins rather than something most people can repeat.

If you have a big following, an auction is a fun, low-risk experiment. If you don't, there's usually no one to bid.

Do branded clothing and ambassador programs pay?

Sometimes, but usually in free products, discount codes or commission on sales rather than cash for wearing a logo. Most ambassador programs expect you to post about the brand, so they're really influencer marketing on a small scale.

Be wary of any "ambassador" program that asks you to buy a starter kit or hit sales targets. That's a buy-in model, not a paid advertising deal.

Which option is best for you?

Pick based on what you already have and what you're willing to commit:

  • You work from cafés, campuses or coworking spaces: a laptop sticker. It's no cost, no audience and no commitment. Start with how it works.
  • You drive a lot and want a fixed monthly amount: an established car wrap program, with a careful eye on scams.
  • You have a real following: an auction or marketplace listing could be worth a try.
  • You love a brand and already post about it: an ambassador program might suit you.

Whatever you choose, the same golden rule applies: legitimate advertising programs pay you, and they never ask you to pay or move money first.

Questions people ask

Is laptop sticker advertising better than a car wrap?
It depends on your routine. Laptop stickers cost nothing, need no car and peel off any time. Car wraps can pay a fixed amount but need lots of driving and carry a high scam risk.
Do I need followers to get paid for a laptop sticker?
Not with Lidquid. You're paid per verified scan from people around you in public, so no social media audience is needed.
Are car wrap advertising programs legit?
Some are, but fake car wrap offers are a well-known scam. Never deposit a cheque and send part of it back to an installer or graphics company.
Is it worth paying a listing fee to sell laptop sticker space?
Only if you're confident someone will buy, usually because you have a following. Otherwise you may pay the fee and sell nothing.
Can I do more than one of these at once?
Often, yes. Just check each program's terms. Some sticker or wrap programs require exclusive space during a campaign.
What's the safest way to get paid to advertise?
Choose programs that are free to join, name the brand up front, explain exactly how you're paid and never ask you to send money.
